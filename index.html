Substitution Guide

One missing ingredient shouldn't stop you from cooking. Most recipes have simple swaps using what you already have on hand.

No buttermilk? Use 1 tablespoon (Tbsp) lemon juice or vinegar + enough milk to make one cup. No fresh garlic? Use 1/8 teaspoon (tsp) garlic powder per clove.

Scrambled Eggs and Toast

Ingredients:

2 large eggs
1 tbsp milk
Salt and pepper, to taste
1 tsp butter
2 slices bread

Instructions:

Whisk eggs in medium-sized bowl with milk, salt, and pepper until fully combined.
Melt butter in a small or medium nonstick pan over low heat.
Pour in eggs and stir slowly and constantly with a spatula.
Remove from heat when eggs are just set but still soft.
Toast and butter the bread, then serve alongside the eggs.

Substitution Tip: No milk? Use water instead! The eggs will still turn out fluffy.

Difficulty: Beginner

Grilled Cheese

Ingredients:

2 slices bread
2 slices cheese (cheddar or American)
1 tbsp butter, softened

Instructions:

Butter one side of each bread slice.
Place one slice butter-side down in a cold pan, add cheese, and top with the second slice butter-side up.
Turn heat to medium-low and cook 3–4 minutes per side until golden and the cheese is melted.
Flip carefully and press down slightly with the spatula.
Let cool for 1 minute before cutting.

Substitution Tip: No butter? Mayonnaise spread on the outside of the bread browns just as well.

Difficulty: Beginner

Garlic Butter Pasta

Ingredients:

8 oz pasta
3 tbsp butter
3 cloves garlic, minced
Salt and pepper, to taste
Grated parmesan cheese

Instructions:

Boil pasta in salted water according to package directions; reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
Melt butter in a pan over medium heat, add garlic, and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
Add drained pasta to the pan and toss to coat, adding splashes of reserved pasta water as needed.
Season with salt, pepper, and top with parmesan.

Substitution Tip: No fresh garlic? Use ⅛ tsp garlic powder per clove.

Difficulty: Beginner

Ground Beef Tacos

Ingredients:

1 lb ground beef
1 packet taco seasoning (or homemade blend, see substitution)
¼ cup water
Taco shells
Toppings: lettuce, cheese, tomato, salsa, sour cream, etc.

Instructions:

Brown ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks, about 6–8 minutes.
Drain excess fat.
Add taco seasoning and water, stir, and simmer 3–5 minutes until thickened.
Warm taco shells according to package directions.
Fill shells with beef and desired toppings.

Substitution Tip: No taco seasoning packet? Mix 1 tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, and salt to taste.

Difficulty: Beginner–Intermediate

Vegetable Stir Fry

Ingredients:

2 cups mixed vegetables (bell pepper, broccoli, carrots, mushrooms, baby corn, water chestnuts, or whatever you have on hand)
2 tbsp oil
2 tbsp soy sauce
1 clove garlic, minced

Instructions:

Heat oil in a large pan or wok over high heat.
Add harder vegetables first (carrots, broccoli) and cook 3–4 minutes.
Add softer vegetables (bell peppers, mushrooms, baby corn, water chestnuts) and garlic, cook 2–3 minutes more.
Add soy sauce and toss to coat, cooking 1 additional minute.
Serve over pre-made rice, if desired.

Substitution Tip: No soy sauce? Use Worcestershire sauce or a splash of vinegar with a pinch of salt.

Difficulty: Intermediate (requires basic knife skills)

Sheet Pan Chicken and Vegetables

Ingredients:

4 chicken thighs or breasts
2 cups chopped vegetables (brussels sprouts, potatoes, carrots, onion)
3 tbsp olive oil
Salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika

Instructions:

Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
Toss vegetables in half the oil and seasonings, spread on a sheet pan.
Season chicken with remaining oil and seasonings, and place on the same pan.
Roast 25–30 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F internal temperature and vegetables are tender.
Let chicken rest 5 minutes before serving.

Substitution Tip: No fresh vegetables on hand? Frozen vegetables work! Add them halfway through roasting, so they don't get soggy.

Difficulty: Intermediate
